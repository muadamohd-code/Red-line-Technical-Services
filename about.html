About Red Line

الخط الأحمر — a Civil Defense approved fire and life-safety contractor built on one principle: every system we design, install and maintain has to pass inspection before it ever needs to save a life.

Who We Are

Red Line Technical Services

Founded in 2010, Red Line Technical Services (الخط الأحمر) is a fully licensed fire and life-safety contracting company operating across Dubai, Sharjah and Ajman. We design, supply, install and maintain fire alarm systems, firefighting systems, CCTV networks, air conditioning units and produce the MEP shop drawings required for Civil Defense approval.

Every engagement begins with the right drawings and ends with a stamped certificate — because the paperwork matters as much as the hardware. We handle Civil Defense fees, exclusion clauses and emirate-specific compliance requirements so clients don't have to.

Our Story

15+ years of licensed fire and life-safety work

2010

Founded in Ajman

Red Line was established and licensed by Civil Defense in Ajman, beginning with fire alarm installation and maintenance for residential and commercial buildings.

2013 – 2016

Expansion into Sharjah & Dubai

Civil Defense approvals were secured for Sharjah and Dubai, enabling full-service coverage across all three northern UAE emirates with emirate-specific compliance on every proposal.

2017 – 2020

MEP & Shop Drawing Division

A dedicated drafting division was launched to produce Civil, Mechanical and Electrical shop drawings — fire alarm layouts, sprinkler plans, HVAC schematics and single-line diagrams for government approval.

2021 – Present

Six Full Service Divisions

Today Red Line operates six divisions: Fire Alarm, Firefighting, CCTV & Security, A/C, MEP Shop Drawings and Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) — serving owners, contractors and consultants across the UAE.
