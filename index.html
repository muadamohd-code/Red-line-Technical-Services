FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are you approved by Civil Defense?

Yes. Red Line is approved and licensed by Civil Defense in Dubai, Sharjah and Ajman.

Which areas do you cover?

We supply and install fire alarm, firefighting, CCTV and A/C systems across Dubai, Sharjah and Ajman.

What is the difference between a zone and a loop fire alarm system?

Zone systems show which area is in alarm. Loop (addressable) systems identify the exact device. See the comparison on our Services page.

Do you offer annual maintenance contracts (AMC)?

Yes. We offer AMC and combined supply, installation and maintenance agreements with scheduled visits.

Can you prepare shop drawings for approval?

Yes. We prepare fire alarm, HVAC, sprinkler and single-line shop drawings for government approval.
