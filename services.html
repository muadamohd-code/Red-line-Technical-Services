Division 01

Fire Alarm Systems

A fire alarm system is designed to alert a building the moment there's an emergency, giving people time to evacuate and giving our technicians and yours time to act. We design, install and maintain both panel types found across the UAE:

ZONE — Conventional Systems

Detectors and call points wired in groups (zones), reporting back to a conventional panel. Cost-effective for smaller buildings where zone-level location is enough.

LOOP — Addressable Systems

Each device carries its own address on the loop, so the panel identifies the exact detector or call point in alarm — the standard for larger and multi-tenant buildings.

Division 02

Firefighting & Suppression Systems

We supply a complete range of fire protection systems, manufactured and installed in line with international standards, matched to the hazard the space actually presents.

Suppression Equipment

From wet-pipe sprinklers in commercial buildings to FM200 clean-agent systems protecting server rooms, every suppression solution we install is matched to the exact hazard profile of the space.

All systems are supplied, installed and commissioned by our own certified team — no sub-contractors, no split accountability.

WATER-BASED

Sprinkler Systems

Wet-pipe and pre-action sprinkler layouts for offices, retail and residential buildings.

GAS-BASED

FM200 & Clean Agent

Clean-agent suppression for server rooms, electrical rooms and archives where water damage isn't an option.

FOAM-BASED

Foam Suppression

POWDER-BASED

Rapid knock-down for electrical and mechanical plant rooms.
